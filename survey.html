Survey

What did you learn?

These ten questions can be used as a survey for students who have read the guide. They cover confidence, specific skills, and which parts of the site helped.

  1. Before using this site, how comfortable were you with business dining situations?

    Rating, 1 to 5

    • 1 – Very uncomfortable
    • 2 – Uncomfortable
    • 3 – Neutral
    • 4 – Comfortable
    • 5 – Very comfortable

  2. After using this site, how comfortable do you feel now?

    Rating, 1 to 5

    • 1 – Very uncomfortable
    • 2 – Uncomfortable
    • 3 – Neutral
    • 4 – Comfortable
    • 5 – Very comfortable

  3. Which topic taught you the most?

    Choose one

    • Preparing before the meal
    • Reading a place setting
    • Ordering and eating
    • Conversation
    • The bill and follow-up

  4. I can explain which utensil to use first and where my bread plate and glass go.

    Agreement, 1 to 5

    • 1 – Strongly disagree
    • 2 – Disagree
    • 3 – Neutral
    • 4 – Agree
    • 5 – Strongly agree

  5. I know what to order, and what to avoid, at a business meal.

    Agreement, 1 to 5

    • 1 – Strongly disagree
    • 2 – Disagree
    • 3 – Neutral
    • 4 – Agree
    • 5 – Strongly agree

  6. I know when to bring up business and when to stick to small talk.

    Agreement, 1 to 5

    • 1 – Strongly disagree
    • 2 – Disagree
    • 3 – Neutral
    • 4 – Agree
    • 5 – Strongly agree

  7. Which feature of the site helped you most?

    Choose one

    • The table-setting diagram
    • The video
    • The self-check quiz
    • The Do and Avoid lists
    • The news and tips

  8. How many quiz questions did you answer correctly on your first try?

    Choose one

    • 0 to 3
    • 4 to 6
    • 7 to 8
    • 9 to 10
    • I did not take the quiz

  9. What is one thing you will do differently at your next business meal?

    Short answer

  10. What was confusing or missing, and what should be added?

    Short answer

Using these questions. Copy them into a form tool such as Google Forms or Microsoft Forms. Questions 1 and 2 use the same scale, so you can compare comfort before and after.
