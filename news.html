October 27, 2026Site update

Welcome to Table Ready

This guide is for students with little or no experience at business meals. Start with Before the meal, or jump to the part you need most.

October 27, 2026New feature

The self-check quiz is live

Answer ten questions and get instant feedback with a short explanation for each. It takes about three minutes. Try the quiz.

October 27, 2026Your turn

Tell us what you learned

After you have read the guide, answer the questions on the survey page. They help show which parts of the site worked and which need more explanation.

October 27, 2026Tip

Practice the table once at home

Set a place at your kitchen table using the diagram, with BMW in mind. Doing it once with real forks makes the pattern stick far better than reading about it.

October 27, 2026Heads-up

Look for etiquette dinners on campus

Many universities run career-center etiquette dinners where students practice with real courses. For example, Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi hosts a series, described on its news site (opens in a new tab). Ask UA Career Services (opens in a new tab) whether something similar is offered near you.
